About Me

I am an M.S. student at Sichuan University. My research interests lie in computer vision and embodied intelligence, with a focus on vision-language-action (VLA) models for robotic manipulation.

My recent work studies how to make VLA policies more efficient and reliable during execution. In particular, I investigate policy-level efficiency and adaptive action execution: extending reliable action horizons, reducing redundant physical steps, and deciding when a robot should act or replan.

For research discussions, please feel free to get in touch.

Research Interests

My work connects visual understanding with reliable and efficient robot action.

Vision-Language-Action Models
Multimodal policies that connect visual observations and language instructions to robotic actions.
Efficient & Reliable Execution
Policy efficiency, action chunking, adaptive execution horizons, and feedback-driven replanning.
Reinforcement Learning for Robotics
Post-training objectives that improve action reliability and reduce redundant execution.
